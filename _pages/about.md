I am Md. Rashed, a **Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the [University of Brahmanbaria](https://uob.edu.bd/cse/employees/md-rashed/)** and a researcher in **computer vision, medical image analysis, and deep learning**. I hold a B.Sc. (Engr.) in **Information and Communication Engineering** from [Pabna University of Science and Technology](https://pust.ac.bd/), where my thesis developed **SE-UNet: Spatial and Channel Refinement** for Robust Multi-Anatomical Structure Segmentation in Abdominal Ultrasound.

At the University of Brahmanbaria, I teach **Advanced Object-Oriented Programming** (theory and sessional), **Computer Programming Techniques**, and **Web Technology** (theory and sessional). Course details are on the [Teaching](/teaching/) page.

Alongside teaching, I continue my research as a **Research Assistant** at [BioRAIN Lab](https://biorainlab.net/author/md.-rashed/). Previously, I was an **Assistant Researcher at PUST** (2024–2025). My work covers AI, computer vision, biomedical image processing, and IoT, including attention-based networks, fully convolutional and hybrid deep models, and machine learning frameworks for real-world applications.

My research has been published in **Healthcare Technology Letters** and **Nutrients**, and at **IEEE FMLDS**, **CIEES**, **QPAIN**, **COMPAS**, and **ICCIT**. Further manuscripts on ultrasound segmentation and classification are under review at **Biomedical Signal Processing and Control**, **IEEE Access**, **Diagnostics**, and the **Journal of Medical Imaging and Radiation Sciences**. I have contributed to a **UGC-funded AI-based real-time fire and accident detection system**, working on data preprocessing, model development, and system integration. My paper on colorectal polyp segmentation (Da-RDU-Net) received the **Best Paper Award at IEEE CIEES 2025**.

I also serve the research community as a reviewer for **SN Computer Science (Springer Nature)** and **ICEEI 2025**, and I am an **IEEE member**.

I have been awarded a **Fully Funded Master's Scholarship in Computer Science at Kocaeli University, Turkey**, but the study has not commenced due to visa constraints, along with multiple **University Merit Scholarships (2021–2023)**.

I am passionate about conducting impactful research and publishing in top-tier journals (**TMI, PAMI, MIA, TIP**) and conferences (**CVPR, MICCAI, MIDL, ISBI, ICLR, ECCV**). With strong expertise in Python, PyTorch, and large-scale experimentation, my goal is to advance state-of-the-art solutions in medical imaging, computer vision, and AI-driven systems.
